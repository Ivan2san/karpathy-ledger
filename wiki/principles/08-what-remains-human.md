# Part 8: What remains human

**P44. You can outsource your thinking, but you cannot outsource your understanding.** The line Karpathy keeps returning to. Even when agents do the work, the human is the bottleneck on knowing what is worth building, what question matters, what result is suspicious, and what tradeoff is acceptable. `[SEQ-B, SEQ-T 28:05]`

**P45. Build it or you do not understand it.** Reading, slides, and blog posts give surface knowledge. Building forces you to confront what you do not know. The only way to genuinely understand something is to build it from scratch without copy-pasting. `[DWA 00:28:00]`

**P46. Re-explain to deepen.** If you cannot explain something simply, you do not understand it. Use the act of teaching as a debugging tool on your own knowledge. `[DWA 02:20:00]`

**P47. The human's job is the brief, not the code.** In AutoResearch the human edits `program.md` and the agent edits the code. The unit of human work shifts from code to the brief that directs the agents. The brief itself becomes the artefact you optimise. The most concrete demonstration of what "spec and plan are the new code" means in practice. `[AR]`

**P48. The leverage now is in designing research orgs, not doing research.** A research organisation is a set of markdown files describing roles, processes, and how the whole thing connects, including "fewer stand-ups because they are useless". You can imagine tuning that org-as-code, having multiple orgs with different risk appetites, and meta-optimising over which brief makes the fastest progress. The human's frontier moves from experimenter to org designer. `[AR, NP 21:43]`

**P69. The few bits that agents cannot do are now your domain.** On microGPT: the agent understands the 200-line distillation but cannot come up with it. The simplification, the taste, the obsession-driven reduction is the human contribution; everything else (explaining, translating to learners, generating examples) agents do better. The strategic question is no longer "what should I learn?" but "which few bits of taste or judgement are uniquely mine, and how do I leverage agents for everything else?" `[NP 1:04:00]`

**P70. Education is being re-routed through agents.** Karpathy: "I'm not explaining to people anymore. I'm explaining it to agents." The agent becomes the router that translates curated taste and structure into individualised explanation. This generalises: documentation, training material, and internal knowledge transfer are all shifting from "explain to humans" to "explain to agents who explain to humans". `[NP 1:02:50]`
