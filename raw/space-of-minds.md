# The Space of Minds

- Tag: SoM

- Date: 30 Nov 2025
- Weight: direct

## Summary
The philosophical floor of the corpus. Lays out the four optimisation pressures shaping animal intelligence and the four shaping LLMs, and argues the optimisation pressure matters most because it dictates the asymptote. LLMs are humanity's first contact with non-animal intelligence, shaped by commercial rather than biological evolution.

## Key claims
- Intelligence is a large space; animals are one point (P104).
- Four animal pressures, four LLM pressures, all different (P105, P106).
- "Failing does not mean death" explains jagged intelligence (P107).
- Commercial evolution, not biological evolution, shapes the asymptote (P108).
- First contact with non-animal intelligence (P109).
- Optimisation pressure matters most, above substrate and algorithm (P110).
- Good internal models give predictive edge (P111); the asymptotic question reframed (P112).
