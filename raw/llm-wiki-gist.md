## Summary
A pattern for a persistent personal knowledge base maintained by an LLM. Three layers (raw, wiki, schema), three operations (ingest, query, lint), and the core insight that RAG rediscovers while the wiki accumulates. Designed for solo research at roughly 100 to 10,000 documents. This ledger is itself an instance of the pattern.
