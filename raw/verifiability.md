## Summary
The theoretical scaffold under the jagged-intelligence claim. Software 1.0 automates what you can specify; Software 2.0 automates what you can verify. A task is amenable to the new paradigm if its environment is resettable, efficient, and rewardable. AI as a new computing paradigm, framed against the 1980s forecasting question.

## Key claims
- The two paradigm rules: specify (1.0), verify (2.0) (P16).
- AI is a new computing paradigm, not a new technology (P17).
- The 1980s analogy: specifiability then, verifiability now (P18).
- The three-part environment test: resettable, efficient, rewardable (P21).
- Non-verifiable tasks fall back on generalisation magic or imitation (P12).
