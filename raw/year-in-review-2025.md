# 2025 LLM Year in Review

- Date: 20 Dec 2025
- Weight: direct

## Summary
Karpathy's own synthesis of the year as five paradigm shifts: RLVR as a new training stage, the ghosts/jagged-intelligence framing, the Cursor-style LLM app layer, Claude Code as AI that lives on your computer, vibe coding, and Nano Banana as a hint of the LLM GUI. Closes with calibration: smarter and dumber than expected, and less than ten percent of capability realised.

## Key claims
- RLVR is the load-bearing 2025 shift; test-time compute is a new scaling law (P92, P93).
- The LLM app layer is real; the lab-versus-app value-capture bet (P94, P95).
- Localhost beats cloud for agents in the current era (P96).
- Vibe coding terraforms software; code is ephemeral (P97).
- Nano Banana hints at the LLM GUI; LLMs replay 70s and 80s computing (P98, P99).
- Power to the people: technology diffusion inverts (P100).
- Benchmark trust collapse (P101); the smarter-and-dumber paradox (P102); under 10 percent realised (P103).
