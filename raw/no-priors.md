## Summary
Karpathy in live exploratory mode after the December inflection and AutoResearch. Covers the "AI psychosis" of unbounded leverage, tokens as the new flops, macro actions, the Dobby home-automation agent, why most apps should not exist, the ecosystem question (independent versus frontier-lab), open source positioning, speciation, untrusted distributed research, and education re-routed through agents. Published 20 March 2026, about two months before his move to Anthropic (19 May 2026).

## Key claims
- AI psychosis; tokens are the new flops; move in macro actions (P57, P60, P63).
- Remove yourself from the bottleneck; trust gating; personality and reward calibration (P62, P65, P64).
- Structural overproduction of apps; people want personas (P61, P68).
- Physical robotics lags; the interface is where value sits; information markets missing (P66, P67).
- Cross-domain generalisation is not happening at the claimed rate; the PhD-and-child superposition (P59, P58).
- Ecosystem: cycling is safest; open source 6 to 8 months behind; speciation bottleneck; untrusted distributed research (P71 to P74).
- The few bits agents cannot do; education re-routed through agents (P69, P70).
