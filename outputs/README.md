# outputs/

Authored, point-in-time deliverables generated FROM the ledger: reports, briefings, teaching artefacts, slide narratives. These carry selection, audience, and "so what" framing, so they are not the same as the wiki (which is faithful synthesis of Karpathy's published work).

Naming: `outputs/YYYY-MM-DD_short-name.md`.

EXPORT-ONLY RULE: outputs flow one way, out. They NEVER feed back into `wiki/principles/`. The ledger's value is single-author purity; a briefing is your framing of Karpathy's ideas, not a Karpathy principle. If writing a report surfaces a genuinely new Karpathy insight, it enters only via the normal ingest path (re-reading a source per AGENTS.md), never from the act of writing the report. Applied or business-specific framing does not belong here at all; it belongs in a private repo.

Note: a faithful reorganisation of existing principles is closer to a wiki page than an output; outputs are for audience-framed, judgement-added deliverables.
